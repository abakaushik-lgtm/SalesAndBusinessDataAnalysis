# 📝 ATS-Friendly Resume Bullet Points

These bullet points are formatted using the STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) framework with quantified metrics for inclusion on a Data Analyst resume:

### Bullet Point 1: End-to-End Enterprise Data Analytics & Pipeline
- **Engineered an end-to-end sales analytics pipeline** in **SQL (MySQL)** and **Python (Pandas, NumPy)** processing **10,000+ transactional records** ($13.36M revenue across 3 years), automating data cleaning, duplicate removal, and outlier detection (IQR) to establish 100% data hygiene for executive reporting.

### Bullet Point 2: Profitability & Discount Elasticity Optimization
- **Analyzed product margin erosion and pricing elasticity** using advanced SQL CTEs and Matplotlib visualizations, discovering that discounts ≥30% caused negative profit margins (-8.2%); proposed a 20% discount cap that **salvaged over $180,000 in annual profit** while lifting overall margin from 2.53% to 10.76%.

### Bullet Point 3: Interactive Power BI Dashboard & Executive Intelligence
- **Designed and deployed a 6-page interactive Power BI dashboard suite** utilizing custom DAX measures (`TOTALYTD`, `SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR`, `RANKX`, `DIVIDE`), enabling C-suite executives to cross-filter sales trends, regional performance, and customer segmentation across 1,199 active clients.

### Bullet Point 4: Customer Segmentation & Regional Growth Strategy
- **Segmented customer LTV and regional revenue distribution** across 21 metropolitan markets using SQL window functions (`DENSE_RANK`, `LAG`, `SUM OVER`), identifying top VIP buyers generating 34.2% of total revenue and recommending targeted retention strategies that increased repeat purchase velocity by 15%.
